🌸 Osaka, Japan

First impressions of Osaka hit you all at once—a vibrant collision of sights, sounds, and smells. The energy is palpable the moment you step out of Umeda Station. It's a city that feels less polished than Tokyo, wearing its soul on its sleeve with a certain gritty pride. You notice it in the dazzling, chaotic river of neon that floods the Dōtonbori canal at night, reflecting off the water in a million shimmering fragments. There's a distinct lack of pretense here. People move with a purpose, but there's laughter—loud, unguarded laughter—echoing from izakayas. You see it in the giant, three-dimensional mechanical crabs and dragon signs that seem to burst from building facades, a testament to the city's unapologetic love for spectacle. It feels real, immediate, and utterly unforgettable from the very first glance.

🐱 Erbing, Shanghai

If you ever visit my home, the first thing you'll likely see is a lazy, orange-colored cloud parked directly in the middle of the hallway. That cloud is Erbing, my three-year-old Garfield cat. Named with the Chinese character for "two" and "cake"—a nod to his round, flat face—Erbing is the absolute monarch of this household, ruling from his plush throne (which is usually just the sunniest spot on the carpet). Weighing in at a solid twelve pounds, Erbing is gloriously, unapologetically fat. When he walks, his low-slung belly sways gently from side to side, and his cheeks are so full they look like two small steamed buns permanently stuck to the sides of his head. His favorite hobby is sleeping in positions that defy the laws of physics, often with his head dangling off the edge of the cat tree and his paws twitching as he dreams of chasing the treats he'll demand as soon as he wakes up.
